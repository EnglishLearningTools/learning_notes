Kenton Festival Guide: A Celebration of Arts and Culture

【肯顿艺术节全攻略：百年庆典、传统舞蹈、烟花盛宴】

Summary: The Kenton Festival, running from May 16th to 19th, features a vibrant lineup including an opening ceremony with a mayoral speech, performances by army cadets and a community play honoring scientist Helen Tongate’s centenary. Attendees can enjoy fireworks, art college videos on intergenerational relationships, and traditional dance demonstrations. Concerts by professionals and local children round out the festivities, with tickets available online or at local shops.

摘要： 肯顿艺术节将于5月16日至19日举行，活动包括市长致辞的开幕式、陆军学员表演和纪念科学家海伦·汤盖特百年诞辰的社区话剧。游客还可观赏烟花、艺术学院的祖孙主题视频展以及传统舞蹈表演。专业音乐家和本地儿童的音乐会为压轴项目，门票可在线或当地商店购买。
