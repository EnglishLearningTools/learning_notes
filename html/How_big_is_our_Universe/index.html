Summary: Powers of Ten: A Journey Through Scale and Time. This video pays homage to the iconic 1977 film by Charles and Ray Eames, taking viewers on an exponential voyage from a picnic in Sicily to the edge of the observable universe. It illustrates the scale of the cosmos, from human dimensions to interstellar space, highlighting key astronomical milestones. The journey showcases advancements in space exploration, such as the Voyager missions and the Hubble Telescope, and concludes with the cosmic microwave background radiation—the oldest light in the universe. The film invites reflection on how our understanding of the universe may evolve by 2077.
