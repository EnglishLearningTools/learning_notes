Business Values Lecture: Collaboration, Hard Work, Creativity, and Excellence

【剑桥雅思12｜听力真题 Test 5 Section 4｜商业讲座揭秘：协作陷阱·无效勤奋·创意误区·卓越迷思】

Summary: This lecture critiques four commonly accepted business values: collaboration, hard work, creativity, and excellence. It argues that these values can backfire if not properly managed, providing examples of teamwork causing inefficiency, hard work wasting resources, creativity failing to boost sales, and the pursuit of excellence missing market opportunities. The speaker emphasizes the need to apply these values strategically rather than dogmatically.

摘要： 本次讲座批判了四种常见的商业价值观：协作、勤奋、创意和卓越。演讲者指出，若管理不当，这些价值观可能适得其反，并通过团队合作降低效率、无效勤奋浪费资源、创意未能提升销量、过度追求卓越错失商机等案例加以论证。强调应战略性地而非教条式地应用这些价值观。
