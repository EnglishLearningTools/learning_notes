The Great Wall: A Monument Through Time

【TED科普：万里长城永不倒：为什么长城这么特殊？】

Summary: The Great Wall of China, originally built by feudal states and unified by Emperor Qin Shi Huang, spans over 13,000 miles as a defense against northern invasions. Constructed by conscripted laborers and soldiers, it became a symbol of suffering with countless deaths recorded during its expansion. Though breached by Mongol invaders, the Ming Dynasty reinforced it with brick watchtowers featuring advanced signaling systems. After the Qing Dynasty rendered it obsolete, sections were repurposed for WWII defense. Now a UNESCO World Heritage Site, it attracts millions of tourists despite preservation challenges, and remains a cultural icon despite the myth about its visibility from space.

摘要： 中国长城最初由战国诸侯修建，经秦始皇连接扩建，形成超过1.3万英里的北方防线。这座由强制征召的劳工和士兵建造的巨墙，在历代扩建过程中成为无数劳动者血泪的象征。虽曾被蒙古铁骑攻破，明朝仍用砖石加固并建起配备烽火台的防御体系。清朝建立后长城军事功能衰退，但二战期间仍被用作抗日防线。如今作为世界文化遗产，尽管面临旅游损耗和"太空可见"的误读，它仍是人类文明史上的奇迹。
