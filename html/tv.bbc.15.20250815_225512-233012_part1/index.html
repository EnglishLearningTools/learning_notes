Summary: The first face-to-face meeting between U.S. President Trump and Russian President Putin since 2022 extended over three hours, focusing on ending the Ukraine war. Trump emphasized demands for a ceasefire, while Putin’s pre-summit attacks on Ukraine cast doubt on his willingness to compromise. The White House framed the talks as “pursuing peace,” but Ukrainian officials expressed skepticism, citing exclusion from negotiations and ongoing Russian aggression.
