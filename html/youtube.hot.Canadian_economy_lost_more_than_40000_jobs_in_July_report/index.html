Canada's July Jobs Report: Worst Numbers in Seven Years

【加拿大青年失业率破14%｜失业潮席卷全国｜贸易战重创核心产业】

Summary: Statistics Canada's July jobs report revealed a shocking loss of 41,000 positions - the worst non-pandemic decline in seven years. Youth unemployment surged past 14%, the highest since 2010, with 34,000 young workers losing jobs. Over half of economic sectors shrank, particularly construction and export-reliant industries hit by US trade wars. Economists now predict a 50% chance of Bank of Canada rate cuts amid rising unemployment.

摘要： 加拿大统计局7月就业数据令人震惊：非疫情期间单月流失4.1万个岗位，创七年最差纪录。15-24岁青年失业率突破14%，达2010年以来峰值。16个行业中有10个出现岗位缩减，建筑业和受美加贸易战影响的出口行业遭受重创。随着失业率持续攀升，经济学家预测加拿大央行9月降息概率达50%。
