Safety precautions include maintaining distance from sharp tools and wearing sturdy shoes due to uneven terrain. The farm features a maze, fish pool, café in a historic schoolhouse, and workshops for traditional crafts like woodwork. Picnic areas are available, and visitors can admire the private Fiddy House from a distance.
