Summary: Thailand's constitutional court has removed Prime Minister Pattongtarn Shinawatra from office due to ethics violations in handling a border dispute with Cambodia. The ruling cited her informal phone conversation with Cambodia's former leader Hun Sen, where she allegedly overstepped her authority. This decision triggers political uncertainty, with potential leadership changes and early elections.
