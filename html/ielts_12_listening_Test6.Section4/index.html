Workplace Conflict: Causes, Effects, and Solutions

【职场冲突全解析：从霸凌到CEO焦虑 | 3大诱因揭秘 | 企业如何化危为机】

Summary: This lecture explores workplace conflict, defining it as unacceptable behaviors ranging from arguments to violence. Causes include power dynamics, personality clashes, and structural issues like team rivalries. Conflict harms individuals through stress and damages companies by reducing productivity. CEOs may exacerbate conflicts due to mixed traits of confidence and anxiety. Solutions involve respectful management, HR mediation, and independent arbitrators.

摘要： 本讲座剖析职场冲突，将其定义为从争执到暴力的不可接受行为。冲突根源包括权力失衡、性格冲突及团队对立等结构性问题。此类冲突会通过压力伤害员工，并因生产力下降损害企业。研究发现CEO因混杂自信与焦虑的特质可能激化矛盾。解决途径涵盖尊重式管理、人力资源部门干预及独立调解等方案。
