Summary: Shona Ferguson from Barford Town Council outlines significant changes over the past 50 years: reduced bus services, pedestrianized zones, and thriving cycle paths. Shopping habits shifted toward nearby cities, while medical facilities consolidated. Education emerges as the top employment sector, with manufacturing surprisingly stable. Current plans include expanded parking, a relocated cinema, and library hour extensions.
