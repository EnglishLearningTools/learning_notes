Wildfire Ravages Southwest France: Unprecedented Destruction

【法国南部野火肆虐：巴黎两倍面积被毁】

Summary: A rapidly spreading wildfire in southwest France has consumed an area larger than Paris, destroying forests and villages. Over 2,000 firefighters are battling the blaze, but conditions may worsen due to wind and extreme dryness. This single fire has already surpassed France’s total wildfire destruction in 2024 and doubled 2023’s damage. Climate change is increasing fire risks in the Mediterranean region, with experts calling it an unprecedented disaster.
