The man who tried to eat every animal on Earth

【BBC精选：饕餮地质学家的狂想人生：吃遍万物、发现巨齿龙、破解地球密码】

Summary: In the 1830s, renowned British scientist William Buckland hosted unconventional dinners featuring exotic animals like mice and crocodile. As Oxford's first geology professor, he conducted groundbreaking fossil research while maintaining unorthodox teaching methods. Buckland's investigation of hyena caves challenged biblical timelines, and he published the first scientific account of a dinosaur. His wife Mary contributed significantly as a scientific illustrator. Despite failing to eat every animal species, Buckland demonstrated that science could be both revolutionary and entertaining.

摘要： 19世纪30年代，英国科学家威廉·巴克兰以老鼠和鳄鱼等异国食材举办奇特宴会。作为牛津大学首位地质学教授，他在开创性化石研究的同时保持着非常规的教学方式。通过对鬣狗洞穴的研究，他质疑了圣经的时间线，并发表了首份恐龙科学报告。其妻子玛丽作为科学插画家贡献卓著。尽管未能尝遍所有动物，巴克兰证明了科学既具革命性又充满趣味。
