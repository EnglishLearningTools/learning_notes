Israel says Hamas chief Mohammed Sinwar has been killed

【以色列称哈马斯领导人穆罕默德·辛瓦尔已被击毙】

Summary: Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced that Mohammed Sinwar, a senior Hamas leader in the Gaza Strip, was killed in an airstrike. Sinwar was the brother of Yahya Sinwar, the former Hamas leader and one of the planners of the October 7 attack. His death came just after Gaza’s first aid distribution center opened on Tuesday, following Israel’s three‑month blockade. Footage from the scene showed chaotic scenes as crowds scrambled for food. Hamas’s leadership has been severely weakened, although the organization typically has the capacity to replace its leaders quickly. Nevertheless, Hamas now faces a more dire situation, with multiple senior figures eliminated across various locations. The aid distribution was marred by violence due to organizational chaos, and humanitarian agencies have criticized the plan’s lack of experience.
