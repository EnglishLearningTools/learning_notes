Summary: The highly anticipated summit between U.S. President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Anchorage concluded without a ceasefire agreement for Ukraine. Both leaders emphasized "progress" but provided no concrete details. Putin praised the talks as "productive," while Trump avoided questions, signaling unresolved tensions. The meeting, lasting over two hours, focused on Ukraine but yielded no immediate breakthroughs. Observers noted Putin’s diplomatic gains, while Trump’s reticence raised questions about the summit’s outcomes.
