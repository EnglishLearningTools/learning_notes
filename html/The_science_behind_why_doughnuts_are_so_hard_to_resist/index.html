Summary: Why do we crave certain foods like burgers and cakes? This video explores the neurological processes behind food choices, focusing on the brain's reward system. MRI studies show that the orbital frontal cortex lights up when we consume foods high in fat and sugar. External factors like food labels and social influences significantly alter our perception of taste. Understanding these mechanisms can help design healthier foods and improve dietary choices.
