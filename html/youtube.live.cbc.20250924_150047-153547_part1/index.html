Former Bank Employee Charged with Accessing Prime Minister's Banking Profile

【CBC News 20250925 加拿大总理卡尼财务数据被窃取】

Summary: Canadian authorities have charged a 23-year-old former Royal Bank of Canada employee, Ibrahim El-Hakim, with multiple offenses including fraud over $5,000 and unauthorized computer use. The RCMP alleges he accessed banking profiles of prominent figures like Prime Minister Mark Carney and created fake accounts for an individual using the alias "AI World" on Telegram, believed to be linked to organized crime. El-Hakim reportedly received $500 per fake account created. While the Prime Minister has not commented, the Finance Minister emphasized the need for robust privacy protections. The RCMP asserts no national security information was compromised, and the suspect is set to appear in court next week.
