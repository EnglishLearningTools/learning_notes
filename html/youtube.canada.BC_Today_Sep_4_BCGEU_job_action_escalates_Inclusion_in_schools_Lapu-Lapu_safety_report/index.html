Summary: The BC General Employees Union (BCGEU) has escalated strike actions, with pickets expanding to Ministry of Finance offices in Vancouver. Representing 34,000 workers, the union demands an 8.25% wage increase over two years plus cost-of-living adjustments, rejecting the province's offer of less than half that amount. Premier David Eby acknowledges significant public sector negotiations this year but emphasizes fiscal constraints, noting each 1% wage increase costs $500 million. With BC facing a record $10 billion deficit, the dispute highlights tensions between fair compensation and economic reality as teachers and nurses prepare for their own negotiations.
