【加拿大新闻 20250823 加拿大宣布取消多种美国商品的报复性关税｜总理卡尼称目前拥有的贸易协议是最好的｜新闻发布会全文】

Summary: Canada's Prime Minister Mark Kerney announced the removal of retaliatory tariffs on US goods covered under the CUSMA trade agreement, effective September 1st. He stated that Canada currently has the best trade deal with the US, with an average tariff rate of 5.6% compared to a global average of 16%, and over 85% of trade being tariff-free. The government will retain tariffs on strategic sectors like steel, aluminum, and autos while intensifying negotiations with the US on these issues. A new comprehensive industrial strategy will be announced to protect Canadian jobs and boost competitiveness. The PM emphasized adapting to the new reality of US trade policy and moving from reliance to resilience by building domestic strength and developing new international markets.

摘要： 加拿大总理马克·科尔尼宣布，自9月1日起取消针对《美墨加协定》(CUSMA)涵盖的美国商品的报复性关税。他表示，加拿大目前拥有与美国最好的贸易协议，平均关税税率为5.6%，远低于全球16%的平均水平，超过85%的双边贸易享受零关税。加政府将保留对钢铁、铝和汽车等战略产业的关税，同时加强与美方的相关谈判。加政府即将宣布一项新的综合产业战略，以保护加拿大就业并提升竞争力。总理强调，加方需适应美国贸易政策的新现实，实现从依赖到韧性的转变，通过增强国内实力和开拓新国际市场来构建G7中最强劲的经济。
