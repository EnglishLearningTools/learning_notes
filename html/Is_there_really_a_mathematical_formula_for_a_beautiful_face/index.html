Is there really a mathematical formula for a beautiful face?

【BBC精选：漂亮的脸真的有数学公式吗？】

Summary: The Golden Ratio, a mathematical concept discovered by Fibonacci, has been hailed as the key to beauty in nature, art, and even human faces. It appears in phenomena from tornadoes to sunflowers and inspired historical studies linking it to aesthetic preferences. However, modern research debunks many of these claims, showing that the preference for golden rectangles may be biased and that natural spirals don’t always follow the ratio. Despite its appeal in popular culture and cosmetic trends, the golden ratio’s connection to beauty is largely unproven. Ultimately, beauty remains subjective, beyond any mathematical formula.
