The Secret Life of Snowflakes: From Hexagons to Cosmic Laws

【BBC科普精选：雪花的秘密】

Summary: Snowflakes form when water vapor crystallizes around dust particles in clouds, creating a hexagonal structure due to molecular angles. Their branching patterns are shaped by unique atmospheric conditions during descent, ensuring no two flakes are identical. Johannes Kepler’s 17th-century theory about hexagonal efficiency was proven centuries later through molecular science. Though often idealized as perfect stars, most snowflakes are irregularly shaped. Their apparent whiteness stems from light scattering through intricate ice structures, while their symmetry reflects universal physical laws governing everything from crystals to galaxies.

摘要： 雪花由水汽在云层中围绕尘埃凝结而成，其分子键角自然形成六边形核心结构。每片雪花下落过程中经历的温度与湿度差异塑造了独一无二的分支形态。开普勒于1611年提出的六边形空间效率猜想，四百年后才通过水分子结构得以验证。尽管常被理想化为对称星形，多数雪花实为不规则颗粒。其白色外观源自光线在冰晶间的散射效应，而对称性则揭示了从微观晶体到宏观星系皆适用的宇宙物理法则。
