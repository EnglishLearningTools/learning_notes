The King's Christmas Broadcast 2024

【国王2024年圣诞致辞】

Summary: The King reflects on the courage of D-Day veterans, the ongoing global conflicts, and the importance of service, compassion, and unity during Christmas, drawing inspiration from faith and humanitarian efforts.

摘要： 国王回顾了诺曼底登陆老兵的勇气、当前全球冲突的严峻局势，以及在圣诞节期间强调服务精神、同情心与团结的重要性，并从信仰和人道主义行动中汲取力量。
