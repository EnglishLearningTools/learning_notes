H1B Visa: Everything You Need to Know

【在美国的华人们都在讨论的H1B签证，你知道它是什么吗？】

Summary: The H1B visa is a popular U.S. work visa for specialty occupations requiring bachelor's degrees. It involves an annual lottery due to high demand, with only about 25% selection rate. Applications are submitted in March for employment starting October 1st. The visa allows three-year renewable stays up to six years and can lead to green card sponsorship. Employers must sponsor applicants, and certain organizations like universities are cap-exempt.

摘要： H1B签证是美国针对专业职业的工作签证，要求申请人拥有学士及以上学位。由于申请量远超名额限制，每年通过抽签机制筛选，中签率仅约25%。申请通常在3月提交，获批者可在同年10月1日开始工作。签证首次有效期三年，可续签至六年，期间可申请绿卡。必须由雇主发起申请，但高校等特定机构可免于名额限制。
