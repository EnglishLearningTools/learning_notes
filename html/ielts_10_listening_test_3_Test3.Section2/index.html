Summary: The Dolphin Conservation Trust, founded 10 years ago, aims to protect dolphins globally by raising awareness about pollution and other threats. The charity focuses on education, visiting schools nationwide, and has 35% of its members as children. It campaigns for fishing policy changes and plans to hire a full-time dolphin biologist. Volunteers support various activities, and the trust recently won an award for its educational work. In Scotland, the trust opposes oil exploration that threatens dolphin habitats due to underwater noise.

摘要： 海豚保护信托成立十年，致力于通过提高公众意识来保护全球海豚免受污染等威胁。该组织注重教育，走访全国学校，35%的成员为儿童。他们推动渔业政策改革，并计划聘请全职海豚生物学家。志愿者协助观测及行政工作，去年因其教育项目获奖。在苏格兰，该组织正抗议石油勘探，因水下噪音迫使海豚离开栖息地。
