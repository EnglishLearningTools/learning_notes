America Mourns "The Nicest Judge in the World" Frank Caprio

【“世界上最仁慈的法官”弗兰克·卡普里奥逝世】

Summary: Judge Frank Caprio, known for his Emmy-nominated show "Caught in Providence," passed away at 88 after battling pancreatic cancer. Instead of imposing punishments, he consistently showed compassion and respect to defendants in his courtroom. His heartfelt interactions, often going viral on social media, earned him the title "the nicest judge in the world." Caprio believed kindness could change lives and urged people to embrace compassion until his final moments.
