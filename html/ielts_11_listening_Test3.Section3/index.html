Creative Writing Students Collaborate on Children's Story Project

【剑桥雅思11｜听力真题 Test 3 Section 3｜创意写作课程大揭秘：森林奇遇×插画创作×团队协作——海伦与杰里米的童书创作之旅】

Summary: Helen and Jeremy, two Creative Writing students, discuss their collaborative project involving writing and illustrating children's stories. They review illustrations for Jeremy's story "The Forest," suggesting dynamic additions like a boy approaching a cave and a tiger. Helen proposes refining crowd scenes and ice-skating sequences with scarves for motion effects. Later, they plan their project report, dividing sections on planning, story inspiration, and illustrations while excluding peer interpretations.

摘要： 海伦和杰里米是创意写作课程的学生，他们正合作完成一个童书创作项目，包含故事撰写和插画设计。两人审阅了杰里米的故事《森林》插画，提议增加动态元素——如男孩走向洞穴的场景和一只梳毛的老虎。海伦还优化了人群舞蹈和森林滑冰的插图，通过添加围巾强化动感。后续讨论中，他们分工撰写项目报告，涵盖企划过程、灵感来源和插画意义，但排除了故事解读部分。
