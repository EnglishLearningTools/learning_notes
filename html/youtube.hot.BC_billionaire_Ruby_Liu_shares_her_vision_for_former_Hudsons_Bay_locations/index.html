Ruby Leu's Ambitious Plan to Revitalize Canadian Retail with Former Hudson's Bay Spaces

【CBC采访女富豪刘宏伟 豪言收购加拿大Hudson's Bay门店租约】

Summary: Ruby Leu, a mall owner with properties in British Columbia, has secured court approval to take over three former Hudson's Bay department store leases. She aims to acquire 28 such leases nationwide, targeting younger generations with immersive shopping experiences. Leu emphasizes innovation, community events, and learning from Asian retail models like Thailand's service excellence. Despite landlord skepticism about her lack of department store experience, she asserts her financial capability and critiques traditional retail's failure to adapt to the e-commerce era.

摘要： 加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚省购物中心业主Ruby Leu获法院批准接管三家前Hudson's Bay百货门店租约，计划在全国收购28处类似物业。她提出三阶段战略（基础店/铂金店/旗舰店），主打吸引Z世代的沉浸式购物体验，并借鉴泰国服务业的成功经验。尽管房东质疑其百货运营经验不足，Leu以自身财富积累和电商时代新思维回应，强调低成本运营与社区活动结合。她还致信总理提议为年轻创业者提供无息贷款，以提振加拿大零售业竞争力。
