Indonesian Garment Factory Struggles Amid US Tariff War

【关税战冲击印尼服装业：订单骤减、市场转移、未来何去何从？】

Summary: For nearly 30 years, an Indonesian garment factory in Central Java has served major US clothing brands. Recent US tariff hikes, initially set at 32% and later reduced to 19%, disrupted exports and squeezed profits. Factory manager Daddy Muly highlights canceled orders and the need to diversify markets beyond the US. Meanwhile, Chinese manufacturers see little advantage in relocating to Southeast Asia due to similar tariffs. Indonesian officials remain hopeful for better trade terms despite current challenges.

摘要： 近30年来，印尼中爪哇的一家服装厂一直为美国知名品牌供货。然而，美国关税战导致订单骤减，初始32%的关税虽降至19%，仍严重冲击利润。工厂经理Daddy Muly表示正转向日本和欧洲市场以弥补损失。中国制造商因东南亚关税与中国相近，暂无意转移产能。印尼政府虽接受当前协议，仍对重新谈判持乐观态度。
