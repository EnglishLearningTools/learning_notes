Summary: Thor Heyerdahl, a Norwegian explorer, defied conventional wisdom with his radical theories on Polynesian migration. As a child, he rejected hunting in favor of building his own museum, foreshadowing his unconventional path. His early adulthood featured a harsh island survival experiment with his wife, where they tested how extreme environments shape lifestyles. Heyerdahl's most famous expedition involved sailing 4,300 miles on a balsawood raft to prove ancient South Americans could have reached Polynesia – though later disproven, this pioneering "practical archaeology" approach revolutionized research methods.

摘要： 挪威探险家托尔·海尔达尔以颠覆性理论挑战学术传统。童年时期他拒绝打猎，沉迷创建私人博物馆；婚后与妻子在荒岛进行极端环境生存实验。他建造仿古木筏横渡太平洋4300英里，试图证明南美人是波利尼西亚祖先——尽管理论最终被证伪，但这种"实践考古学"方法革新了研究范式。他晚年用芦苇船验证古埃及航海技术，虽屡遭学界质疑，其跨学科探索精神深刻影响了人类学、考古学等领域。
