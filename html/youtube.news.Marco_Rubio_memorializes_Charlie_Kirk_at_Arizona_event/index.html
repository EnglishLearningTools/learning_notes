A Tribute to Charlie Kirk: A Movement for America's Soul

【演讲全文：卢比奥在今天的Turning Point USA上悼念查理·柯克】

Summary: About 10-12 years ago, a political ally introduced Charlie Kirk as a young man starting a movement to promote American exceptionalism and counter Marxism on college campuses. Initially skeptical, the speaker admits being proven wrong as Kirk's movement grew over 16 years. Kirk is remembered for his profound knowledge, wisdom beyond his years, and bold engagement with ideological opponents. His impact reached global proportions, with world leaders offering condolences upon his passing at age 31. The tribute emphasizes Kirk’s messages about marriage, family, and patriotism, while ultimately framing his work within Christian theology—believing in eternal reunion through Christ’s resurrection.
