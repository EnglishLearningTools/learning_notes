Hoarding was a way to protect myself from the world

【BBC Ideas 囤积背后的创伤：一位女性从失去、挣扎到重生的真实自白 | 心理创伤与强迫症 | 社会偏见与救助困境】

Summary: A mental health professional's simplistic advice to "stop buying newspapers" fails to address the complex trauma underlying hoarding behaviors. This firsthand account reveals how the loss of two children triggered decades of compulsive accumulation, isolating the narrator from family and endangering her living conditions. Despite well-intentioned interventions, forced cleanups exacerbated shame until specialized support enabled breakthrough. With 1.4 million affected in the UK alone, the piece highlights hoarding's links to OCD/PTSD and advocates for trauma-informed approaches that prioritize agency over stigma.

摘要： 心理健康专家"停止买报纸"的简单建议无法解决囤积行为背后的复杂创伤。亲历者叙述失去两个孩子的痛苦如何引发数十年强迫性囤积，导致家庭关系破裂与居住安全隐患。尽管干预措施出于善意，强制清理反而加剧了当事人的羞耻感，直至专业支持带来转机。英国约有140万人受此影响，本文揭示囤积行为与强迫症/创伤后应激障碍的关联，倡导用创伤知情方法取代污名化标签，尊重患者自主权。
