Queensland Teachers' Historic Strike: Demanding Fair Pay and Better Conditions

【教师怒吼！澳大利亚昆士兰5万教师大罢工：薪资过低·校园暴力·过劳危机】

Summary: Queensland teachers have staged the largest strike in the state's history, with 50,000 educators walking off the job to protest an 8% pay offer over three years, which they say would make them the lowest-paid teachers in Australia. Beyond wages, teachers cite classroom violence, severe staff shortages, and unsustainable workloads as key reasons for the protest. Despite schools remaining open with skeleton staff, only 13% of students attended, highlighting widespread community support. The union warns of further action if demands are unmet, while the government claims negotiations are ongoing.

摘要： 昆士兰州5万名教师举行史上最大规模罢工，抗议政府提出的三年加薪8%方案，该方案将使当地教师薪资沦为全国最低。除薪资问题外，校园暴力、师资短缺和过重工作量成为抗议主因。尽管学校维持最低限度运营，学生出席率仅13%，显示民众广泛支持。工会警告若诉求未获回应将升级行动，政府则表示仍在协商中。
