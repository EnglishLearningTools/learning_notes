Summary: In a significant move, President Trump has signed a proclamation imposing a new annual $100,000 fee on H1B visa applications, marking the largest overhaul of the program to date. The administration claims this will ensure companies hire only highly skilled foreign workers who do not replace American labor. Additionally, a new "gold card" visa program requires a $1 million investment for individuals or $2 million for businesses to fast-track U.S. residency. The changes have sparked immediate concern among tech companies, with firms like Meta and Microsoft advising employees to avoid international travel due to re-entry risks.
