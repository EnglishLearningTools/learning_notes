Summary: Germany has more billionaires than any other European country, yet income inequality continues to grow. While the wealthy benefit from rising real estate and stock values, a significant portion of the population lives near the poverty line. Manager Magazine annually compiles a list of Germany’s 1,001 richest individuals, though many prefer to remain anonymous. The super rich often avoid public attention due to fears of envy, security risks, and historical stigmas. Despite their visibility in business networks and philanthropy, they generally resist wealth redistribution policies and higher taxes.

摘要： 德国拥有全欧洲数量最多的亿万富翁，但社会贫富差距持续扩大。随着房地产和股票价值飙升，富豪资产快速增长，而普通民众生活水平停滞甚至下降。《经理人杂志》每年发布德国千大富豪榜，但多数富豪选择隐匿身份。出于对公众嫉妒、安全威胁和历史包袱的顾虑，德国超级富豪普遍保持低调。尽管他们活跃于商业社交圈并参与慈善捐赠，但普遍反对财富再分配政策和高额税收提案。
