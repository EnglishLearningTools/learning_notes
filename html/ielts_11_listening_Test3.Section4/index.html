Summary: Ethnography, a research method rooted in anthropology, is increasingly being used in business to gain deep insights into customer needs and behaviors. Companies employ ethnographic studies to design better products, from kitchen equipment to mobile phones, by observing real-world usage. Researchers emphasize open-mindedness and first-hand observation, allowing participants to guide the study rather than testing predefined hypotheses. The method involves extensive data analysis to identify patterns and meaningful insights, which ultimately inform the design process.

摘要： 人种志作为一种源于人类学的研究方法，正被企业广泛运用于挖掘客户需求和行为模式。通过观察真实使用场景，企业成功改进了从厨房量杯到全球手机等功能设计。这种研究强调开放思维和实地观察，让参与者而非研究者主导方向。研究人员需分析海量数据以识别规律，最终将深刻洞察反馈至产品设计流程，典型案例包括为乌干达设计的通话计时功能，以及帮助护士移动查房的平板电脑。
