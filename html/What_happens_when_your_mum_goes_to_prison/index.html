What happens when your mum goes to prison?

【BBC精选短片：如果你的妈妈进监狱了】

Summary: The video reveals the profound trauma children experience when their mothers are imprisoned. A mother recounts her sudden sentencing and the devastating separation from her young children, who were unprepared and emotionally shattered. The children faced foster care separation, difficult prison visits, and long-term psychological scars. Experts emphasize that maternal imprisonment has lifelong impacts on children's health and well-being. The judicial system often fails to consider these consequences, punishing not just the mother but also her innocent children.
