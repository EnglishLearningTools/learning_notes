Summary: French Prime Minister Ponceauat Beixot's government collapsed after losing a parliamentary confidence vote, plunging the nation into political paralysis. He will tender his resignation to President Emmanuel Macron, who intends to appoint a new prime minister in the coming days. The vote against him was triggered by his proposal to cut public spending by 44 billion euros, which lost support in a fractured parliament. This marks the second time in nine months a French prime minister has been ousted by a confidence vote, highlighting the country's deep political divisions and economic challenges.
