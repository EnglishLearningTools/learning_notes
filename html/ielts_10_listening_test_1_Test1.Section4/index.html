Summary: The Spirit Bear, a rare white variant of the Black Bear, is an endangered species native to British Columbia. Local legends attribute special powers to its white fur, and communities protect it by keeping its sightings secret. The bear’s survival depends on old-growth rainforests, where it plays a crucial role in the ecosystem by feeding on salmon and enriching the soil. However, habitat loss due to logging and declining salmon populations threaten its existence. Conservation efforts are underway, but more action is needed to ensure its future.

摘要： 灵熊是黑熊的罕见白色变种，生活在加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚省，目前濒临灭绝。当地传说认为其白色皮毛具有特殊力量，居民对其行踪守口如瓶以保护它。灵熊的生存依赖古老雨林，通过捕食鲑鱼和滋养土壤维持生态平衡。然而，伐木导致的栖息地丧失和鲑鱼数量减少正威胁其生存。尽管保护行动已展开，仍需更多措施确保其未来存续。
